# rppd 0.2.17

2026-11-01. Needs etcds 0.23.

## New

- **Secrets while they rotate.** `SECRET.check(name, presented)` says whether a presented
  value is the secret's. While a secret is rotated, `SECRET.get` gives the new value and
  `SECRET.check` accepts the old and the new one until the old one is discarded.
- **Messages in six languages** (de, en, fr, ja, ru, zh): by the environment's `LC_ALL`,
  `LC_MESSAGES` or `LANG`, or the embedder's choice (`i18n::set_lang`).
- `problems.md`: the known problems, most severe first.

## Changed

- Built on etcds 0.23: a queue whose watch the server cancelled is watched again.
- CI, coverage and audit workflows.
